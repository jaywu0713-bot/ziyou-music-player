自用音乐播放器解码组件说明

最后修改日期：2026-10-20（America/Los_Angeles）

- `@ffmpeg/ffmpeg` 0.12.15 is MIT-licensed.
- `@ffmpeg/core` 0.12.10 is GPL-2.0-or-later and includes additional dependencies under their respective licenses.
- Upstream project and source: [ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm](https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm), release tag [v0.12.10](https://github.com/ffmpegwasm/ffmpeg.wasm/releases/tag/v0.12.10).

The upstream core binary is used without modification. The project patches the JavaScript wrapper in `scripts/patch-ffmpeg-core.mjs`; the patch is applied by `scripts/prepare-decoder.mjs`. During a local build, generated WebAssembly and worker assets are split into static files and reassembled in the browser. Music stays on the user's device.

The corresponding application source, wrapper patch, and build scripts are published at https://github.com/jaywu0713-bot/ziyou-music-player under GPL-3.0-or-later. The generated Pages build is published in the `gh-pages` branch. The original FFmpeg core source remains available from the upstream project linked above.
